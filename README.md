# theorem

A collection of PDFs gathered for study.

- **`pydagoras/`**: generalizations of the Pythagorean theorem. Covers n-dimensional versions (De Gua's theorem, simplices, volumes, Cauchy–Binet), spherical and hyperbolic geometry, Riemannian metrics, distance geometry, and geodesy (distances on the Earth). Includes theses and dissertations.
- **`aitraining/`**: papers on widely used AI and LLM training techniques. Files are numbered by topic: 01 architecture, 02 scaling, 03 model reports, 04 optimizers, 05 efficiency, 06 MoE, 07 data, 08 fine-tuning, 09 alignment/RLHF, 10 reasoning/RL, 11 practical guides and surveys, 12 open training recipes, 13 free textbooks.

All documents are the work of their original authors and remain under their original licenses. Most `aitraining/` files are from arXiv; the arXiv ID is in each filename.
